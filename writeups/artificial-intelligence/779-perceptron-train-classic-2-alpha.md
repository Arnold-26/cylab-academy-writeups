## Énoncé

> Watch a perceptron learn in real time using the classic update rule: only
> misclassified points trigger updates, with no weight decay. In this variant you
> must find `5` successful learning rates (each reaching 100% accuracy) before the
> flag is revealed. The dataset is still linearly separable, but it is less toy-like
> and the separation is less obvious. Dial in a rate, trigger a run, and the
> interface animates each step.
